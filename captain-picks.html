How the captain score works

The score starts from FPL's own expected points for each player's next match. It adds a quarter of the player's recent form, then adjusts for the opponent: easier fixtures add points and harder fixtures take them away. Players flagged as less than 75% likely to play are left out, because a captain who doesn't play scores nothing.

The table also shows ownership. A captain owned by most managers protects your rank if he scores. A less-owned captain can gain you ground if he outscores the popular pick, but it's a bigger gamble.

Before you lock it in

  • Check the injury list again close to the deadline.
  • Pick a vice-captain who is very likely to start, so the armband isn't wasted if your captain misses out.
  • In a double gameweek, a good player with two matches usually beats a great player with one.
