What the flags mean

  • Doubtful (yellow): the player has a knock or illness. The percentage is FPL's estimate of his chance of playing in the next round: 75%, 50% or 25%.
  • Injured: the player is not expected to play in the next round.
  • Suspended: the player is banned, usually after a red card or too many yellow cards.
  • Unavailable: the player has left on loan, been transferred abroad, or is otherwise out of the squad.

Players are sorted by ownership, so the news that affects the most FPL teams comes first. The list comes from the official game and refreshes several times a day, but managers' press conferences often give fresher news in the days before a deadline.

What to do with a flagged player

  • A short-term knock in a good player is usually worth waiting out, especially if you don't have a free transfer.
  • A long-term injury is worth a transfer, because a player who scores zero for weeks costs you more than a points hit.
  • Make sure your bench has players who are likely to play, so automatic substitutions can cover a late absence.
