How to build a balanced FPL squad

The best squads don't spread money evenly. They put it where points come from and save it where they don't.

Spend where the points are

Most of the big hauls in FPL come from attacking players: goal-scoring midfielders and forwards who take penalties and play every minute. That's why most strong squads put a large share of the budget into two or three premium attackers.

Goalkeepers and the fourth or fifth defender rarely decide a gameweek. Many managers spend as little as possible there and use the savings up front.

The three price bands

  • Premiums: the most expensive players in the game. Pick the ones who start every week, take set pieces and have good fixtures. These are your usual captains.
  • Mid-priced players: the engine of a good squad. Look for players who have moved into a more attacking role, or who play for a team on an easy run.
  • Budget enablers: the cheapest players who still start regularly. A cheap defender who plays 90 minutes on a solid team is worth more than an expensive one who is rotated.

Bench: cheap but playing

Your bench only scores when a starter misses out or you play Bench Boost. That makes it a good place to save money. Choose cheap players who are likely to start, so auto-substitutions actually give you points. A bench full of players who never play is money and points wasted.

Don't forget defensive points

Defenders and defensive midfielders can now earn 2 points for defensive contributions (10 or more clearances, blocks, interceptions and tackles for defenders, 12 or more including recoveries for midfielders and forwards). Busy centre-backs on mid-table teams can be good value even without many clean sheets. See how FPL scoring works.

Keep a little flexibility

  • Leave a small amount in the bank, or pick players you could easily swap for a premium later.
  • Avoid three players from one team unless they're all nailed starters with good fixtures. It limits your transfers later.
  • Balance teams by fixtures. Use the fixture grid so your players don't all have hard matches in the same week.
