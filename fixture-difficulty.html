How to read the fixture grid

Each row is a Premier League team and each column is a gameweek. Every cell shows the opponent, H for home or A for away, and is coloured by FPL's own difficulty rating from 1 (dark green, easiest) to 5 (dark red, hardest). A shaded cell with a dash means the team has no match that week, and a split cell means two matches.

The Run column adds up how kind the whole run is: an easiest-rated fixture scores 5 and a hardest-rated one scores 1. Teams with more matches score higher, so double gameweeks push teams up the list.

How to use it

  • Look for defenders and goalkeepers from teams with a long green run. Easy fixtures mean more clean sheets.
  • Look for attackers facing several weak defences in a row.
  • Plan transfers two or three weeks ahead, so you're bringing players in just as their good run starts, not halfway through it.

See blank and double gameweeks for how to plan around weeks when teams play twice or not at all.
