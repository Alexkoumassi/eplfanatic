About EPL Fanatic

EPL Fanatic is an independent site built by a fan, for Fantasy Premier League managers who want the key numbers in one place before every deadline.

The tools read the public data from the Fantasy Premier League game and refresh every hour. Nothing here is a guarantee: prices, team news and points can change at any time, so double-check in the official game before you confirm transfers.
