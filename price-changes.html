How to use the price change watch

Fantasy Premier League prices move overnight, by £0.1m at a time, depending on how many managers buy or sell a player compared with how many already own him. The Rising table lists the players with the most net transfers in this gameweek. They are the most likely to go up in price. The Falling table lists the players being sold the most, who are the most likely to drop.

Ownership matters as much as the raw number. 100,000 transfers in for a player owned by 50% of managers is less pressure than 50,000 for a player owned by 3%. Use the position filter to compare players you're actually choosing between.

When this helps

  • If you already plan to buy a rising player this week, buying before tonight's change saves you £0.1m.
  • If a player you plan to sell is falling, selling before the drop keeps your budget intact.
  • Don't make a transfer only to chase a price rise. Points matter more than £0.1m.
